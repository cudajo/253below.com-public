1. Overview

253below.com ("we," "us," or "our") operates the lojjal service/application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the application.

This policy applies to both types of users on our platform:

  • Customers: Individuals using the App to discover, save, and redeem offers.
  • Merchants/staff: Business owners and their employees using the App to create offers, scan codes, and view dashboard analytics.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

  • Personal Data: When you register for an account, we collect personally identifiable information, such as your email address, phone number (not required) and authentication credentials. For Merchants registrations, we also collect business-related information (store name, logo, brand colors) to create and manage your public-facing Store Profile in lojjal.
  • App Usage & Interactions: We automatically collect usage data to provide our services and understand how the App is used. This includes:
    • Customers: The offers you view, claim to your digital wallet, renew, and redeem.
    • Merchants: The offers you create, analytics on those offers, and offer scanning activity.
  • Location Data: At this time, lojjal does not request, collect, or track your precise geographical location (GPS data). If this changes in the future to introduce location-based offer discovery, we will update this policy and explicitly ask for your permission within the App prior to collecting any location data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account securely.
  • Enable the core functionality of the App (saving offers to digital wallets, processing QR code scans, managing stamp cards).
  • Provide Merchants with anonymized, aggregated analytics regarding their specific campaigns (e.g., total views, total claims, total scans etc.).
  • Process purchases and subscriptions (for Merchant Pro Tiers).
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve the App’s performance.
  • Resolve technical issues and respond to customer service requests.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers that facilitate our core infrastructure. These service providers only have access to the data necessary to perform their specific functions and are legally obligated to protect it:

  • Cloud Hosting & Database Providers: To securely store user accounts, digital wallets, and store profiles.
  • Authentication Services: To safely manage your login credentials and secure your account.
  • Analytics Providers: To track aggregated app usage and engagement metrics, helping us improve the user experience.
  • Crash Reporting Tools: To monitor app stability and collect error reports so we can fix bugs quickly.
  • Payment Processors: To securely manage in-app purchases and subscriptions for our Merchant Pro Tier. We do not process or store your raw credit card information on our servers; this is handled entirely by our secure payment partners and the respective App Stores.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

In-App Deletion: You can delete your account directly within the lojjal app by navigating to your Account Settings and selecting "Delete Account." Executing this action will immediately and permanently erase your account credentials, wallet data, and personal profile from our active databases.

(Special note for Merchants: Deleting an owner account may also remove associated active offers and store profiles to protect customer data integrity).

6. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.

7. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under the age of 16. If you become aware of any data we have collected from children under age 16, please contact us using this email address: info@253below.com to have it removed.
