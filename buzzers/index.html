Buzzers

for iOS & Android

Buzzers is the perfect cross-platform companion app when playing trivia games or having a quiz together with your friends and family. Download Buzzers today and upgrade your next game night!

Product highlights

Big red buzzer

With a big red buzzer button for each player to push, you will always know in what order they buzzed. And most importantly - who was first!

Host your own quiz

Host a quiz and share the room code with your friends using the QR code, web link or manually by a short code.

Offline?

No internet? No problem. Use the app as a simple, standalone buzzer button to make noise. Perfect for casual board games or just for fun!

Big Screen Experience

Buzzers is fully compatible with Google TV & Android TV. Host the game show from your sofa and display the participants buzzing in on the TV for everyone to see, or choose to keep it secret until the round is finished.

Gameplay

Be the ultimate quiz master

As the host (Online Mode), you have total control.
Precision Timing: See the exact order players buzzed in.
Suspense Mode: Hide who buzzed in or their answers until you are ready to reveal them.

Versatile answer types

Buzzers goes beyond a simple red button. Tailor the input to your specific game type:
Classic Buzzer: Big red button for speed rounds.
Yes / No: Ideal for rapid-fire binary questions.
Multiple Choice (A/B/C): Perfect for structured trivia questions.
Free Text: Let players type their answers for "fill in the blank" rounds.

Local and online play

The host and participants don't necessarily have to be in the same place as everything is run online and in real time sync.

Fun & customizable

Express yourself with unique avatars and user aliases. Choose your buzzer sound to match your style — from a professional game show "ding" to a chaotic air horn or car honk to stress your opponents!
